About TeleMed Vision

Our Mission

TeleMed Vision is dedicated to making medical imaging accessible to everyone. We believe that healthcare workers, researchers, and patients should have easy access to medical image viewing tools without the complexity of traditional DICOM software.

Who We Serve

  • Healthcare workers and medical professionals
  • Radiology students and educators
  • Research institutions
  • Small clinics and practices
  • Patients who want to view their own medical images
