Privacy & Cookies Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

At TeleMed Vision, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your medical data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in accordance with HIPAA, GDPR, and other applicable healthcare regulations.

2. Information We Collect

Medical Images and Health Information

We collect and process medical images (X-rays, CT scans, MRIs, etc.) and related health information that you upload to our platform. This information is encrypted and stored securely.

Account Information
  • Name, email address, and professional credentials
  • Institution affiliation and licensing information
  • Usage data and platform interactions
Technical Data
  • IP address and browser information
  • Device information and usage patterns
  • System performance and error logs

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the service: Process, store, and display medical images
  • For security: Monitor for unauthorized access and prevent fraud
  • To improve the service: Analyze usage patterns and user feedback
  • Legal compliance: Meet healthcare regulatory requirements
  • Communication: Send important service updates and security notifications

4. Data Security and Encryption

We implement multiple layers of security to protect your medical data:

  • End-to-end encryption: All medical images are encrypted during transmission and storage
  • Access controls: Role-based access with multi-factor authentication
  • Audit trails: Complete logging of all data access and modifications
  • Secure infrastructure: HIPAA-compliant hosting with regular security audits

5. Data Retention

Medical data is retained according to healthcare regulations and your account settings:

  • Free accounts: 30-day retention with manual renewal
  • Paid accounts: Configurable retention (90 days to 7 years)
  • Deletion: You can request immediate deletion of any medical data

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR and other privacy regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate personal information
  • Deletion: Request removal of your account and data
  • Portability: Export your medical data in standard formats
  • Restriction: Limit processing of your data

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to enhance your experience and secure our platform:

Essential Cookies

Required for basic functionality, including authentication and security:

  • Session management cookies
  • Security tokens
  • Load balancing cookies
Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences and settings:

  • UI preferences and language settings
  • Viewer configuration settings
  • Session state preservation
Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how the service is used (always anonymized):

    li>Feature usage statistics
  • Performance metrics
  • Error tracking and debugging

8. Cookie Management

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, blocking essential cookies may prevent the service from functioning properly. We provide a cookie consent banner that allows you to manage non-essential cookies.

9. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services that comply with healthcare regulations:

  • Cloud infrastructure: AWS and Google Cloud (HIPAA-compliant)
  • Payment processing: Stripe (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Analytics: Custom analytics (no medical data shared)

10. Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach, we will:

  • Notify affected users within 72 hours (as required by law)
  • Provide details about what data was affected
  • Offer guidance on protective measures
  • Implement additional security measures to prevent future incidents

11. International Data Transfers

Medical data may be processed and stored in secure data centers located in different countries. All transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and use appropriate safeguards including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Adequacy decisions where available
  • Technical and organizational security measures

12. Children's Privacy

TeleMed Vision is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify users of significant changes through email or prominent notices on our platform.
