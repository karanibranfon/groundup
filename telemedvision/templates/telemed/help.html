Getting Started

Visit our Pricing & Signup page and choose a plan. Click "Get Started Free" for the free plan or "Start Free Trial" for paid plans. Fill in your details and verify your email address. Your account will be ready in less than a minute!

Once logged in, navigate to your dashboard. Click the "Upload" button and select your DICOM files or medical images. You can also drag and drop files directly into the upload area. The system supports X-rays, CT scans, MRIs, and other common medical imaging formats.

Select the images you want to share, click "Share," and enter the recipient's email address. You can set permissions (view only or download) and expiration dates. All shared images are encrypted end-to-end for maximum security.

Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats are supported?

We support DICOM files, as well as common image formats like PNG, JPEG, and TIFF for medical imaging.

Is my data HIPAA compliant?

Yes, all data storage and transmission is HIPAA compliant with full encryption and audit logs.

Can I access images offline?

You can download images for offline viewing, but the web viewer requires an internet connection.

What browsers are supported?

We support the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Mobile browsers are also supported.

How much storage do I get?

Free accounts get 512MB. Paid plans start at 5GB and go up to 100GB or more for enterprise plans.

Can I collaborate with other users?

Yes, you can share images and reports with colleagues, invite team members, and set up group workspaces.

What are the AI features?

Our AI can help generate preliminary reports, detect anomalies, and provide diagnostic suggestions to assist radiologists.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from your account settings. No questions asked, no cancellation fees.
