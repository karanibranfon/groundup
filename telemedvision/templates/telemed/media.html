Company Overview

About TeleMed Vision

TeleMed Vision is a leading web-based medical imaging platform that enables healthcare professionals to securely view, share, and collaborate on medical images. Founded in 2020, we serve over 10,000 healthcare professionals across 500+ institutions worldwide.

Mission

To make medical imaging accessible, secure, and collaborative for healthcare professionals everywhere, improving patient outcomes through better image sharing and diagnostics.
